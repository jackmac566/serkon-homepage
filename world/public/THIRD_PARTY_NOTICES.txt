Additional environment assets
-----------------------------
PBR surface scans, outdoor HDR lighting and boulder_01 geometry/textures:
Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 (public domain).
https://polyhaven.com/license
See materials/provenance.json and models/boulder/provenance.json for asset pages,
original download URLs and verified hashes. Assets are self-hosted; this website
makes no runtime request to the Poly Haven API.

Interaction references (original application/DOM implementation):
Henry Heffernan: https://github.com/henryjeff/portfolio-website
Bruno Simon: https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025

Vehicle geometry
----------------
Default: Ferrari 458 Italia by vicent091036, CC BY 4.0.
Original model: https://sketchfab.com/models/57bf6cc56931426e87494f554df1dab6
Distributed through Three.js r186 official car example:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r186/examples/webgl_materials_car.html
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: 4.4m scale, grounded tires, steering/wheel animation, wine red paint,
silver wheels, tinted glass. Original GLB bytes unchanged.

Fallback: oldSchool.glb, Copyright (c) 2025 Bruno Simon, folio-2025, MIT.
Draco decoder: Google Draco, Apache-2.0; all decoder files are self-hosted.
Complete licenses, fixed source details and verified hashes:
models/vehicle/LICENSE.md and models/vehicle/provenance.json.
